Read `references/soldier-runtime.md` completely immediately before building every generation request. This Skill must not write a second art direction or a second runtime.

## Panel-specific overlay

Check the shell-module construction explicitly: no more than 200 equal or nearly equal square or near-square fragments, a clear geometric grid with chipped edges, matte natural layers and a dense core dissolving into missing or floating modules. Keep the source brief’s 30%–45% subject scale in the designed region and abundant intentional whitespace. Extract bright, fresh source-derived colours rather than copying muddy tones. Do not accept an ordinary mosaic, game pixels, smooth solid blocks, plastic tiles, strong pearlescence or a fully retained photographic scene as this style.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-113/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
